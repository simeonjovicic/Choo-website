Our Story · 我们的故事

A piece of Asia
in the heart of Vienna

CHOO Foodstore was born from a simple passion: to bring the authentic tastes of Asia to Vienna's food-loving community. What started as a modest family venture has grown into the city's most-loved Asian supermarket — a place where locals and expats alike come to rediscover the flavours of home.

From hand-picked sauces and fragrant pastes to rare teas, fresh produce and celebratory snacks — every shelf tells a story. We travel to source it, so you don't have to.
